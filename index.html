The Verification Loop

How It Works

Six steps from operator action to digital evidence. Each step is deterministic — not probabilistic.

01

Operator performs the step

The operator works normally at their station. No wearables, no special tooling.

02

Camera observes

A mounted camera tracks parts, tools, and hand placement. Runs fully on-premise — no cloud, no latency.

03

Rules verify

A deterministic rule engine checks that the step happened in the correct sequence, location, and duration. No guessing.

04

Exception detected

If something doesn't match — wrong sequence, missing part, obstruction — the system signals immediately. The line is not stopped blindly.

05

Human resolves

The operator or supervisor reviews the exception. Their decision — including the reason — is recorded. Machines verify. Humans decide.

06

Evidence recorded

Every step, every timestamp, every exception and resolution is written to a hash-linked audit record. Available for export at any time.

Human-in-the-Loop

Machines Verify.
Humans Decide.

Elevenified-Vision is designed to support operators and supervisors — not replace them. The system detects the defined assembly condition, signals exceptions, and records human decisions when intervention is required.

The line is never stopped blindly by the system. Every override, confirmation, and supervisor resolution is recorded with credentials and tied to the unit record.

✓

Advisory, Non-Gating Signaling

If camera visibility dips or hands partially block the view, the line never halts blindly. The system escalates to an operator visual check.

✓

Supervisor Review Queue

Uncertain cycles are routed to an asynchronous supervisor review queue — preserving line takt time while maintaining full inspection coverage.

✓

Human Attestation Recorded

Every manual override, confirmation, or supervisor resolution is recorded with user credentials and tied to the permanent unit record.

✓

AI Where It Accelerates. Rules Where It Decides.

AI assists during initial SOP teach-in to draft step definitions with human engineers in the loop. On the live floor, verification runs purely on deterministic, auditable rules — never opaque models.

Technical Questions
Elevenified-Vision Studio is designed so that authoring a new manual assembly SOP takes hours, not months — not weeks of integration work. An engineer records a golden-run assembly, outlines target regions on screen, and the rule compiler generates the station inspection profile. We'll validate actual setup time as part of the pilot.
AI is strictly an acceleration tool during configuration — it never makes verification decisions on your live production line. We use AI during SOP authoring and visual teach-in to help engineers outline target regions, recommend temporal hold times, and generate draft step criteria with human engineers always in the loop. Once compiled into a station profile, verification runs 100% on explicit, deterministic state rules.
The line is never halted blindly by a visual noise event. If confidence drops below a defined threshold, the system flags the cycle for supervisor review rather than stopping production. The operator can continue; the supervisor reviews the flagged cycle asynchronously and records their decision.
Hardware requirements are confirmed during pilot setup and depend on the specific process, workstation geometry, and environment. The system is designed to work with standard, widely-available industrial edge hardware and cameras — not proprietary equipment. We size this together during the pilot scoping conversation.
Elevenified-Vision provides human-readable audit reports and downloadable JSON/CSV data extracts through the Supervisor UI — structured files your team can import into existing MES, SCADA, or Electronic Batch Record systems. Purpose-built, turnkey connectors for specific platforms are on the roadmap, not available today.
The runtime — what workers use at the station during production — is offline-compatible once the device is registered and the process is configured. Initial device registration and SOP configuration require internet connectivity. But these are one-time setup steps. After that, production verification, evidence recording, and operator guidance all run locally with no internet dependency.
